Location & Directions

📍

Thiemeloods

Leemptstraat 34, 6512 EN Nijmegen

Located near the beautiful city center of Nijmegen. A unique, atmospheric venue perfect for our Lustrum celebration!

🚲 Bike Parking Information

  • 🚉 Central Station: Secure bike parking available at Nijmegen Central Station, just a 10-minute walk to the venue.
  • Keizer Karel Plein: City center bike parking is available near Keizer Karel Plein, also about a 10-minute walk away.
  • 🏡 Neighborhood: You may park in the surrounding neighborhood streets, but please note there is no official bike parking and a spot is not guaranteed.
